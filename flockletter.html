The first two get their own series later. This one gets three parts now, because it went from a requirements document on a tray table to production in about a week, and the how is more useful than the what.

If you only read one, read Part 3. It's the one I'd tell a former guest about.

None of this is my day job, and I'm not a professional developer - every agent, dashboard, guardrail, and now app here was built at home (or in transit), nights and weekends, working with AI.
